Delivery Guide

Our store provides delivery services for products available in the catalog.

Delivery Time
Standard delivery usually takes between 2 and 5 business days.
Delivery time can vary depending on the customer's location and product availability.

Delivery Tracking
Customers can use their order information to track the delivery status when tracking is available.

Delayed Orders
If an order is delayed, the customer should contact customer support with the order information.

Delivery Address
Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
An incorrect address may cause delivery delays.

Receiving an Order
The customer should check the package when it arrives.
If the package appears damaged, the customer should report the problem to customer support as soon as possible.

Unavailable Customer
If the customer is not available during delivery, the delivery service may attempt another delivery or contact the customer.
