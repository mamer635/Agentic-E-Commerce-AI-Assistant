Smartphone Troubleshooting Guide

Phone Does Not Turn On
1. Make sure the battery has sufficient charge.
2. Connect the phone to a compatible charger.
3. Wait several minutes.
4. Try restarting the device according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Battery Drains Quickly
Possible causes include high screen brightness, background applications, poor network signal, GPS usage, high refresh rate, gaming, and video streaming.
The customer can review battery usage settings and close unnecessary applications.

Phone Is Overheating
A smartphone may become warm during gaming, video recording, fast charging, hot environmental conditions, or intensive application usage.
If the device becomes unusually hot, the customer should stop demanding applications and allow the device to cool.

Phone Is Slow
Possible causes include too many background applications, low available storage, software issues, or resource-intensive applications.
Customers can restart the device and check available storage.

Charging Problem
Customers should check the charging cable, charger, power outlet, and charging port.
Customers should use compatible charging equipment.

Damaged Product
If a product arrives damaged, the customer should document the damage and contact customer support as soon as possible.

Wrong Product
If the customer receives a product different from the ordered item, they should contact customer support and provide the order information.
