Payment Methods

Our store supports several payment methods.

1. Credit and Debit Cards
Customers can pay using supported Visa and Mastercard credit or debit cards.
The payment must be successfully authorized before the order can be processed.
If a card payment fails, the customer should verify the card details, available balance, and expiration date.

2. Cash on Delivery
Cash on delivery is available for eligible orders and delivery locations.
The customer pays the delivery agent when the order is delivered.
Cash on delivery availability may depend on the order value and delivery location.

3. Online Payment
Customers can complete payment online during checkout.
The order will only be processed after successful payment confirmation.

4. Payment Security
Payment information should not be shared with other people.
The store does not ask customers to send card passwords or PIN numbers through messages.
If a payment problem occurs, customers should contact customer support.
